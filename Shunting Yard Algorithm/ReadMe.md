Definition and history -

The shunting yard algorithm is a classic computer science method used to parse mathematical expressions written in infix notation (e.g., 3 + 4)
and convert them into postfix notation, also known as Reverse Polish Notation (RPN).

Invented by Edsger Dijkstra in 1961, it is named after a railroad shunting yard
because its data-shuffling process closely mirrors how trains are split and routed onto separate tracks.
